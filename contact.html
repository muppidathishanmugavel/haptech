Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS-friendly resume and why do I need one?

Many companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to scan resumes before a person reads them. An ATS-friendly resume uses a clean format and the right keywords so your experience is easy to read for both software and recruiters.

Do you build resumes and portfolios for freshers and students in Tamil Nadu and Kerala?

Yes. We work with students, freshers, job seekers and working professionals in Tamil Nadu and Kerala, including Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Tirunelveli, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram and Kozhikode.

How much does portfolio website creation cost?

The price depends on the number of pages and features you need. Send us your requirement and we will share a quote that fits your budget.

I live outside the big cities. Can I still use HAPTECH?

Yes. Our process runs online through WhatsApp and email, so you can get a resume or portfolio from anywhere in Tamil Nadu or Kerala.

Do you guarantee a job or interview?

No. A strong resume and portfolio improve how you present yourself, but we cannot guarantee jobs, interviews or internships.

Do you provide SEO services in Tamil Nadu and Kerala?

Yes. HAPTECH provides on-page SEO, local SEO, keyword research, technical SEO and backlink building for businesses across Tamil Nadu and Kerala, including Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Tirunelveli, Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram. SEO takes time, and we do not guarantee specific rankings.

Can you build a custom app from my idea?

Yes. Share your idea and we plan, design and build a customized web or mobile-friendly app around it. Our team has built apps such as a Tamil local-store ordering app and a staff attendance and salary manager.

What is the marathon automation app?

It is a customized app that automates a marathon or sports event: online registration, UPI payment tracking, participant database and categories, WhatsApp updates, and results and certificates. Features are planned around your event.
